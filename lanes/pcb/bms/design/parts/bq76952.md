# U1 cell monitor: TI BQ7695204PFBR (BQ76952 family, SPI + CRC, REG1 on at 3.3 V by default)

Datasheet: `datasheets/bq76952.pdf`, TI SLUSE13B (Jan 2020, rev. Nov 2021). Read: §5 device comparison, §6 pins, §7 specs, §12.3 LDOs, §14.3 SPI, §15 balancing, §16 application (Fig. 16-2, Table 16-3).

**Why REG1 must be on by default.** The 5 V buck's EN comes from REG1 (v14-design-notes §9). The plain BQ76952 wakes from SHUTDOWN with REG0 and REG1 **off** (§12.3.2), and RAM configuration is lost in SHUTDOWN. So the board could never power its own ESP32 after a button wake, unless OTP were programmed on the line (§12.3.2 describes over-driving REG1 to do it). The -04 variant comes up with REG1 = 3.3 V from the factory, so a blank board can power its ESP32. **OTP programming is still mandatory** (decision B9, §7): without it the protections and the UV latch exist only after the ESP32 writes them.
⚠️ REG0 on by default for the -04 comes from TI's FAQ (SLUAAQ5 §4.7: REG1-default variants power the MCU without OTP); no TI document states it explicitly (decision B12). Fallback: overdrive the `TP_REG1` pad with 3.3 V and write OTP.

**Cost of SPI** (§6, Table 6-1): SCL → SPI_SCLK, SDA → SPI_MISO, **HDQ → SPI_MOSI, CFETOFF → SPI_CS**. Two of the planned NTC pins are lost.

**NTC split:** the flex has 5 NTCs. The BQ76952 takes **3** (protection-grade, OT/UT in hardware; DCHG is now the charge gate, review B1). Flex NTCs 4 and 5, the board NTC and the box-air NTC go to ESP32 ADC1 ([ntc.md](ntc.md)). ⚠️ NTCs 4 and 5 have no hardware protection.

## 3. Balancing
- R(CB) internal 15–46 Ω (§7.20); with 2 × 20 Ω external: I = 3.4 V / (40 + 28) ≈ **50 mA typ**, max 3.65 / (40 + 15) ≈ 66 mA.
- **External resistor power** at 66 mA: 87 mW each → **ERJ-PA3F20R0V** (0603 anti-surge, 0.33 W; 26 %). ERJ-P03 (0.2 W) misses the ≥ 250 mW rule.
- **BQ heat:** 2 cells at the consistent corners ≈ 0.17 W typical (review n2); the bound that pairs maximum current with maximum R is 0.4 W. RθJA 66 °C/W (§7.4) → ≈ 11 K, 26 K bound.
- **Which cells together:** cells 3 and "16" share node VC3 (VC4–VC15 tied to it), so they're physically adjacent. The TRM balances only "non-adjacent cells in use" (p. 85) and doesn't say whether it counts 3 and 16 as adjacent ⚠️. So **`Cell Balance Max Cells` = 1** and autonomous balancing off in OTP. Host-controlled balancing ignores that limit (TRM p. 175): **firmware rule: ≤ 2 cells, never 3 and 16 together.** Raise the limit only after a bench check.
- 50 mA on a 210 Ah group is slow (0.024 %/h): fine for matched LFP (cells.md). Not a pack-repair balancer.

## 5. Fine print
- Soft-SHUTDOWN: TS2 held low or LD held high at shutdown entry sticks the device (TI FAQ). Momentary button, LD to VSS ✓.
- REGIN must not exceed BAT (internal diode, §12.3.1) ✓.
- The BAT diode and the NPN collector diode must be separate parts (§16.2 bullets, p. 64) ✓.
- The SPI CS falling edge wakes the HFO; the first transaction may need a retry (§14.3) → firmware.
- Factory defaults are unsafe for this pack (TRM p. 154–179): all 16 inputs are cells, CUV off, no temperature protection, `Shutdown Cell Voltage` off, COV 4.35 V. §7 fixes them in OTP.
- Never use DEEPSLEEP: REG1 is off by default there and the button doesn't exit it (p. 52; review n6).
- ⚠️ DDSG and DCHG follow **protection faults**; the pins "assert or deassert" in NORMAL, SLEEP and DEEPSLEEP and are high-impedance in SHUTDOWN (TRM p. 60–61). A CUV recovers by itself (review m1; intent §4 holds the firmware rule).
- Don't send `FET_CONTROL`, `CHG_PCHG_OFF` or `DSG_PDSG_OFF` while the pins are in DCHG/DDSG mode (TRM p. 34). Self-test drives the pins as GPOs instead ([self-test.md](self-test.md)).

### 7.2 Procedure (board only, never with the pack connected)
1. J1 and J3 unplugged. Bench supply **11.3 V**, 100 mA limit: + to J3-1 (B+) **and** `TP_VC16`, − to GND. `TP_BAT` should read ≈ 10.5 V (one diode drop): inside 10–12 V at both nodes ✓.
2. Pulse the button (J8) if `TP_REG1` isn't 3.3 V. Still 0 V → overdrive `TP_REG1` with 3.3 V (≤ 3.6 V). The ESP32 runs from the board (buck EN = REG1) or from USB.
3. ESP32: check the device number → `SET_CFGUPDATE` (0x0090) → write §7.3 **in order, `Vcell Mode` first** → read every value back.
4. Still in CONFIG_UPDATE: `OTP_WR_CHECK` (0x00A0). Byte 0 must be 0x80 (bit 7 only; TRM p. 15). Otherwise stop.
5. `OTP_WRITE` (0x00A1), bit 7 set on success → `EXIT_CFGUPDATE` (0x0092).
6. Power off ≥ 5 s. Fit a **cell simulator** on J1 (4 × 1 kΩ string across the supply; 10 kΩ from each of the three BQ NTC pins to GND), power at **12.8 V** (3.2 V per "cell": above CUV recovery 3.036 V and shutdown; recheck R5). Without it the new cell-voltage shutdown and open-NTC faults act within seconds.
7. Verify without writing: all registers read back from boot; `TP_REG1` 3.3 V; **DDSG and DCHG high with no fault** ⚠️; open one NTC (UTC) → DCHG low; lower one tap below 2.78 V → DDSG low after 2 s; below 2.60 V → SHUTDOWN after 5 s; ESP32 held in reset → auto shutdown after 10 min.

**RAM trial first (first board only; recheck R1, R5):** before step 3's OTP path, fit the cell simulator on J1, supply **12.8 V** (3.2 V per cell, above CUV recovery 3.036 V), write §7.3 in RAM, exit CONFIG_UPDATE, wait ≥ the CUV delay, and run step 7's checks **without any power cycle** (RAM is lost on one). DDSG and DCHG must go high with no fault (FET_CTRL_EN set, Mfg Status Init FET_EN = 1). Only then: unplug the simulator, supply 11.3 V, and do steps 3–5 for real.
