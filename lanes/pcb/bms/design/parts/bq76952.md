**Why REG1 must be on by default.** The 5 V buck's EN comes from REG1 (v14-design-notes §9). The plain BQ76952 wakes from SHUTDOWN with REG0 and REG1 **off** (§12.3.2), and RAM configuration is lost in SHUTDOWN. So the board could never power its own ESP32 after a button wake, unless OTP were programmed on the line (§12.3.2 describes over-driving REG1 to do it). The -04 variant comes up with REG1 = 3.3 V from the factory: **no OTP step at all.** OTP stays optional, e.g. to store LFP protection thresholds so they apply from the first millisecond after wake ⚠️ (decide in firmware design; the ESP32 rewrites them within ≈ 1 s of boot anyway).

**Cost of SPI** (§6, Table 6-1): SCL → SPI_SCLK, SDA → SPI_MISO, **HDQ → SPI_MOSI, CFETOFF → SPI_CS**. Two of the planned NTC pins are lost.

**NTC split:** the flex has 5 NTCs. The BQ76952 takes 4 (protection-grade, OT/UT in hardware). NTC 5, the board NTC and the box-air NTC go to ESP32 ADC1 channels. Changes intent §4: 6 → 4 BQ thermistors.

## 3. Balancing
- R(CB) internal 15–46 Ω (§7.20); with 2 × 20 Ω external: I = 3.4 V / (40 + 28) ≈ **50 mA typ**, max 3.65 / (40 + 15) ≈ 66 mA.
- **External resistor power** at 66 mA: 87 mW each. A standard 0603 (100 mW) runs at 87 %, which breaks the ≤ 50 % rule. Use **anti-surge 0603 at ≥ 250 mW** (Panasonic ERJ-PA3 or similar ⚠️ MPN at schematic; 35 %) or 0805.
- **BQ heat:** 2 non-adjacent cells (autonomous limit) × 66² mA² × 46 Ω max ≈ 0.4 W worst case; typical 2 × 70 mW. RθJA 66 °C/W (§7.4) → **≈ 9 K typ, 26 K worst.** Cap simultaneous cells at 2 in config.
- 50 mA on a 210 Ah group is slow (0.024 %/h): fine for matched LFP (cells.md). Not a pack-repair balancer.

## 5. Fine print
- Soft-SHUTDOWN: TS2 held low or LD held high at shutdown entry sticks the device (TI FAQ). Momentary button, LD to VSS ✓.
- REGIN must not exceed BAT (internal diode, §12.3.1) ✓.
- The SPI CS falling edge wakes the HFO; the first transaction may need a retry (§14.3) → firmware.
- Default protections after wake are TI's factory defaults (Li-ion values ⚠️). Until the ESP32 writes LFP thresholds (≈ 1 s), protection runs on defaults → consider OTP (§1).
